# Agentic BI Retail Dataset

This project uses a retail business dataset designed for
AI-powered Business Intelligence and strategic recommendation.

## Dataset Components

The dataset contains the following related tables:

- `customers.csv` — Customer information and lifetime metrics
- `products.csv` — Product information, pricing, and costs
- `orders.csv` — Customer orders and order-level information
- `order_items.csv` — Products and financial metrics associated with orders
- `inventory.csv` — Product inventory snapshots
- `marketing_campaigns.csv` — Marketing campaign spending and revenue
- `returns.csv` — Product returns and refund information
- `business_scenarios.csv` — Business scenarios for testing AI insights

## Purpose

The dataset is designed to support:

- Natural-language business queries
- SQL generation
- Business data analysis
- Trend analysis
- Interactive visualization
- AI-generated insights
- Strategic recommendations
- Business Intelligence reports

## Business Scenarios

The data contains positive, negative, and neutral business conditions,
including:

- Strong growth
- Margin compression
- Marketing inefficiency
- Overstock risk
- Stockout risk
- Customer loyalty
- Returns problems
- Regional opportunities
- Stable performance
- Loss-making products

## Data Type

This is a **retail business dataset generated for academic
and research purposes**. It does not represent confidential or
proprietary data from a real company.
